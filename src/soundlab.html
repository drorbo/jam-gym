Sound Lab

Candidate sounds for the Keys panel's Guitar and Organ timbres (the two synthesised ones rock and blues lean on). Nothing here is wired into the app yet — click around, then say which ones you like by name and they'll go in.

Guitar

What rock's Keys panel plays by default today, and three alternatives. Each has a clean and an amped (overdriven, cabinet-filtered) version — flip the switch and replay.

Organ

What blues and rock can both switch to today, and one richer alternative (more drawbars, a touch of Leslie wobble).

"Play chord" plays a G7 comping voicing (G3 B3 D4 F4). Guitar cards also have "Play muted chug" — four quick palm-muted eighth notes on the root, the rock rhythm-guitar move. The Wurlitzer and Grand piano choices aren't here: they're real recordings already (credits), not synthesised, so there's nothing to improve by ear.
