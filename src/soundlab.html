Sound Lab

Candidate sounds for the Keys panel's Guitar and Organ timbres (the two synthesised ones rock and blues lean on). Nothing here is wired into the app yet — click around, then say which ones you like by name and they'll go in.

Guitar

What rock's Keys panel plays by default today, two real recordings, and three synthesised alternatives. Every card has an Amped (overdriven, cabinet-filtered) switch — flip it and replay, including on the recordings.

Organ

What blues and rock can both switch to today, and one richer alternative (more drawbars, a touch of Leslie wobble).

"Play chord" plays a G7 comping voicing (G3 B3 D4 F4). Guitar cards also have "Play muted chug" — four quick palm-muted eighth notes on the root, the rock rhythm-guitar move; the two real recordings play an actual choked, muted take for that, not just a shortened ringing note. The Wurlitzer and Grand piano choices aren't here: they're real recordings already (credits), already good, so there was nothing to compare them against.
